// about

A small studio, not an agency.

10BIT LABS LTD is a UK-registered software studio. We design, build and publish our own apps and software products, rather than working as contractors for other companies.

Our approach is simple: pick a problem worth solving, build something well-considered around it, and keep it maintained after launch. We'd rather ship a handful of apps we're proud of than a long list of half-finished ones.

Each app we release is treated as its own product, with its own audience and its own privacy policy and terms, hosted where it lives — App Store, Google Play, or its own website.
