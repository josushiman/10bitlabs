Sıra is a score tracker for Turkish card and tile games. It is provided by 10BIT LABS LTD ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Sıra iPhone or iPad app (the "App").

## Summary

Sıra is designed to work offline. We do not operate user accounts or a server for the App, and we do not collect your match data. The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Your match information is stored locally on your device. If you choose to buy the one-time Unlock, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores the information needed to keep score and preserve your match history, including:

- player or team names you enter;
- game and variant selections;
- match dates and status;
- scores, rounds, and game events;
- whether matches are active or archived;
- the number of free matches started on that installation; and
- a local cache indicating whether Apple has verified the Unlock for that device.

This information is stored in the App's local database. We cannot access it, and the App does not send it to us or to an analytics provider.

You may choose to enter a person's name as a player or team name. Please use nicknames or other non-sensitive labels where appropriate and do not enter information you do not have permission to use.

## Apple services and in-app purchases

Sıra uses Apple's StoreKit framework to request the local App Store price, process an optional purchase, restore purchases, redeem eligible offer codes, and verify whether an Apple Account is entitled to the Unlock. Apple, not 10BIT LABS LTD, processes the transaction and associated payment and account information. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account credentials.

The App reduces verified StoreKit information to a local unlocked/not-unlocked state. Apple's handling of information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Backups

Sıra does not provide account-based or CloudKit synchronisation. However, the App's local data may be included in an iCloud backup or a device backup if you enable those system services. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us. Restoring a device backup may restore the App's local match data.

## Tracking, advertising, and analytics

We do not use your information to track you across apps or websites. The App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, profiling, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Retention and deletion

Match data remains on your device until you delete the relevant match or delete the App. Deleting a match permanently removes that match, its player or team names, and its rounds from the App's local database. You may also rename a player or team within the App.

The free-match tally and local Unlock cache cannot be cleared separately in the App. They are removed when the App is deleted. Deleting the App does not cancel, refund, or erase an App Store purchase associated with your Apple Account. Copies contained in a device backup remain subject to your backup settings and Apple's retention practices.

Because we do not hold your local match data on a server, we cannot view, export, correct, or delete it on your behalf.

## Security

Your match data benefits from the security protections provided by your device and operating system. You are responsible for controlling access to your device and maintaining any backups you require. No storage method is completely secure, so Sıra should not be used to store confidential or sensitive personal information.

## Children's privacy

Sıra does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user. All match information is entered and stored locally as described above. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of App Store purchasing features.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights concerning personal information. We do not possess or control the match information stored on your device. You can manage that information directly in the App by renaming a player or team or by deleting a match. You can remove all of the App's local information by deleting the App, subject to any device backups you retain.

For questions about information processed by Apple, contact Apple or use the privacy controls associated with your Apple Account.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Sıra, the law, or our practices. We will update the effective date when we do. If a change materially affects how the App handles information, we will provide notice through an appropriate channel, such as the App, its App Store listing, or the page where this policy is published.
