Grid is an iOS app in development for keeping home measurements close at
hand. It's being built for the moment you're measuring a window at home, choosing
materials in a shop or checking whether a piece of furniture will fit.

## Measurements with a place

The planned notebook brings multiple properties, their houses, gardens and sheds
into one place. Organise measurements by room, or save items directly within an
area. Each window, door or piece of furniture gets its own page with labelled
dimensions, notes and photos.

## From a room to a whole floor

Build room plans from manually measured walls, doors and windows. Add furniture
using simple measured shapes, then position and rotate it to explore the space.
Connect adjoining rooms through shared doors or openings to build a whole-floor
overview. Plans and item pages will share the same recorded dimensions.

## Your measurements, in your units

Grid is being designed for metric and imperial measurements, including feet,
inches and fractions. Switch viewing units while keeping the original entry
available. Estimates will be clearly marked, and a missing dimension will stay
“Not measured”.

## Find the right dimension

Search across properties, areas, rooms and items, with filters to narrow the
results. The aim is to make a saved dimension easy to find again, whether you're
at home or out shopping.

## An offline notebook

The first version is planned to work without a login or internet connection,
with measurements and photos kept on your phone. Capture, editing and search
will work offline.

Manual JSON export and import are planned for recovering structured notebook
data. Photos will remain local and won't be included in those exports.
