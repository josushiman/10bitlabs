Sıra is an offline score tracker for Gonga, Okey and Okey 101. Your matches are
stored locally on your device.

## Purchases and the Unlock

Sıra includes three free games. After that, a one-time £2.99 Unlock lets you
start as many games as you like. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

Apple processes App Store payments, Family Sharing, purchase restoration and
refund requests. Sıra does not receive your payment-card details or Apple
Account credentials.

## Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase, then open the
Unlock offer in Sıra and select **Restore purchase**.

If Sıra does not find a purchase, check that you are using the Apple Account
used for the original transaction. If it still does not unlock, [contact Sıra
support](mailto:sirasupport@10bitlabs.co.uk).

## Refund and billing help

Apple handles App Store charges and refund requests. Request a refund through
your Apple Account or the App Store.

Apple reviews refund requests under its own policies and applicable law. Sıra
cannot approve, decline or process a refund.

Contact Sıra support only if the problem concerns how the app recognised the
purchase after you tried **Restore purchase**.

## Moving to a new device

The Unlock can ordinarily be restored on an eligible device using the Apple
Account that made the purchase.

Match data is separate from the Unlock. Sıra does not provide account-based or
CloudKit sync. Your local data may be included in an iCloud or device backup if
you use those Apple services.

## Report a problem

Email [sirasupport@10bitlabs.co.uk](mailto:sirasupport@10bitlabs.co.uk) with:

- What happened and what you expected instead
- The steps leading to the issue
- Your device model, iOS version and Sıra version
- Screenshots with personal information removed where possible

Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, verification codes or
payment-card details. We aim to reply within five working days, in UK English.

Reply to the existing email conversation when following up. Support requests do
not receive reference numbers.
